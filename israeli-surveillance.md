### The Unit 8200 Pipeline

A 2018 Haaretz study estimated 80% of the 2,300 founders of Israel's 700+ cybersecurity companies came through IDF intelligence. Unit 8200 (equivalent to NSA) functions as an incubator - soldiers develop surveillance capabilities for military use, then commercialize them after service. This military-to-startup pipeline is the source of most Israeli surveillance technology sold globally.

## Spyware / Mobile Exploitation

### NSO Group (Pegasus)
`US Blacklisted` `Spyware` `Mobile`

- **Location:** Herzliya, Israel
- **Founded:** 2010 by Shalev Hulio and Omri Lavie (Unit 8200 alumni)
- **Product:** Pegasus spyware - zero-click mobile surveillance tool that can extract messages, emails, photos, and activate microphones/cameras remotely.
- **Notable:** Used by governments to target journalists, activists, dissidents. Used to spy on Jamal Khashoggi before his murder. Ordered to pay $167M to Meta. Blacklisted by Biden administration in 2021.

### Paragon Solutions (Graphite)
`Spyware` `Zero-Click`

- **Location:** Israel
- **Founded:** 2019 by Ehud Schneorson (former Unit 8200 commander) and Ehud Barak (former Israeli Prime Minister)
- **Product:** Graphite spyware - extracts data from encrypted messaging apps (WhatsApp, Signal, Telegram) via zero-click exploits.
- **Notable:** WhatsApp disrupted 2025 campaign targeting ~90 journalists. DEA confirmed user. Trump admin reactivated $2M contract. Customers: Australia, Canada, Cyprus, Denmark, Israel, Singapore.

### Candiru
`US Blacklisted` `Spyware`

- **Location:** Tel Aviv, Israel
- **Founded:** By Unit 8200 alumni
- **Product:** Spyware sold to governments for targeting Windows, macOS, iOS, Android devices.
- **Notable:** Blacklisted by Biden administration in 2021. Citizen Lab found Candiru spyware targeting activists, journalists, academics, embassy workers, and dissidents in multiple countries.

### Cytrox / Intellexa
`US Blacklisted` `Spyware`

- **Location:** Israel / Cyprus / Barcelona

### QuaDream
`Spyware` `iOS`

## Key Observations

**Unit 8200 Pipeline:** Israeli Defense Forces signals intelligence unit (equivalent to NSA). Functions as incubator for private surveillance companies. Veterans have founded 1,000+ companies. Military training in offensive cyber capabilities directly commercialized.

**Mobile Phone Focus:** The majority of Israeli surveillance companies target mobile phones (Pegasus, Graphite, Candiru, Predator, Cellebrite, Circles). The phone is the richest surveillance target - always on you, has your location 24/7, stores all communications, has microphone/camera, contains contacts/network map, holds photos/notes/passwords. One successful phone compromise yields more intel than months of traditional surveillance. Israel has cornered this market.

**Palestinians as Test Subjects:** Multiple companies (notably AnyVision, Elbit, Cyberbit) have used occupied Palestinian territories as a "testing ground" for surveillance tech before exporting globally. The occupation functions as an R&D lab - technologies proven against Palestinians are marketed as "battle-tested" to foreign governments.

**Export to Authoritarians:** Israeli surveillance tech has been sold to Malaysia, Botswana, Azerbaijan, Angola, Honduras, Peru, Nigeria, Ecuador, Mexico, Ethiopia, Kazakhstan, Trinidad and Tobago, Colombia, Uganda, UAE, Saudi Arabia, and many others. These tools consistently end up targeting journalists, activists, and dissidents. Pegasus was used to spy on Jamal Khashoggi before his murder.

**US Relationship:** Despite blacklisting NSO Group, Candiru, Cytrox, and Intellexa (2021-2023), US agencies (ICE, CBP, FBI, DEA, DHS) remain major buyers of Israeli surveillance tech - particularly Cellebrite, Voyager Labs, Cobwebs. The blacklist applies to specific companies, not the ecosystem.

**Corporate Structure:** Many companies use complex multi-jurisdiction structures (Israel/Cyprus/Bulgaria/Luxembourg) to obscure ownership and evade regulation. Intellexa Alliance is prime example - nominally "European" but Israeli-founded and operated.
