### Predatorgate: Europe's Spyware Capital Scandal

Greece is not a classic "Unit 8200 pipeline" exporter. It became the **European operating theater** for the **Intellexa** mercenary-spyware consortium and the site of **Predatorgate** — the EU's highest-profile democratic spyware crisis. Israeli-founded tooling (Predator, built on Cytrox tech) was procured and used against Greek journalists, opposition politicians, and others while Intellexa entities operated from Greek corporate structures. The case produced rare criminal accountability: a 2026 Athens first-instance conviction of Intellexa founder **Tal Dilian** and associates. Greece therefore belongs in this repo as both a **host jurisdiction for commercial spyware ops** and a **buyer/abuser** of the same class of tools.

## Intellexa Alliance — Full Corporate Stack

Citizen Lab and PEGA reporting describe **Intellexa** as a marketing label for a **constellation of mercenary surveillance vendors** assembled under Tal Dilian from 2018–2019 — not a single product company.

### Intellexa S.A.
`predator` `mercenary-spyware` `entity-list` `greece`

- **Location:** Greece (Intellexa S.A.); multi-jurisdiction web (Ireland, Hungary, North Macedonia, Cyprus, France)
- **Founder:** Tal Dilian (former Israeli military intel / Unit 81–linked commander; also historically tied to other cyber-offensive ventures)
- **Partner / corporate specialist:** Sara Aleksandra Fayssal Hamou (business partner; co-defendant in Athens case)
- **Product:** **Predator** (also tracked as **Alien/Predator** implant family) — commercial mercenary spyware giving operators deep access to target phones (messages, cameras, microphones, files) after infection, often via malicious links / zero-days.
- **Notable:** US Commerce **Entity List** (July 2023) added **Intellexa S.A. (Greece)** and **Intellexa Limited (Ireland)**. US Treasury sanctioned the **Intellexa Consortium** and **Cytrox AD** (March 2024). Greek DPA fined Intellexa €50,000 (2023) for obstructing the Predatorgate inquiry. Greece is where the consortium's political blowback hit criminal court.

### Cytrox (Cytrox AD / Cytrox Holdings)
`predator` `north-macedonia` `hungary` `entity-list`

- **Location:** Founded North Macedonia (2017); Cytrox Holdings Zrt (Hungary); acquired into Intellexa stack
- **Product:** Core **Predator** mobile spyware technology (Android-focused origins; later iOS/Android agency-grade suite). Cisco Talos documented Alien/Predator implant evolution after the "rescue" acquisition.
- **Notable:** Entity List (2023) hit **Cytrox AD (North Macedonia)** and **Cytrox Holdings (Hungary)**. Treasury sanctions (2024) on Cytrox AD. Product lineage = Balkan R&D + Israeli capital/talent; Greece became sales/ops/legal node.

### WiSpear / Passitora Ltd.
`intellexa` `wifi-intercept` `cyprus` `alliance`

- **Location:** Cyprus-linked (Passitora / WiSpear); Intellexa alliance member
- **Product:** Wi-Fi / network interception and related surveillance tooling marketed into government markets; Dilian vehicle that helped absorb Cytrox (2018–2019 reporting).
- **Notable:** PEGA and investigative outlets map WiSpear as one of the four named Intellexa building blocks (with Cytrox, Nexa, Poltrex/Senpai lines). Part of the EU-shell strategy that put Greek and Cypriot soil under PEGA scrutiny.

## Predatorgate Timeline (Core Facts)

### Targets & discovery
`journalists` `opposition` `eyp`

- **2022:** Financial journalist **Thanasis Koukakis** discovered Predator infection; opposition figure **Nikos Androulakis** (and others) reported targeting attempts. Parallel lawful wiretaps by the Greek National Intelligence Service (**EYP**) on journalists deepened the crisis.
- **Meta / Citizen Lab:** Confirmed Predator infections; later reporting included dual US-Greek national and former Meta security policy manager **Artemis Seaford** infected while in Greece (*New York Times*, 2023).
- **Hellenic DPA:** Investigation found Predator-polluted SMS links sent to **92+ phone numbers** (reporting variants cite ~220–350 messages) targeting journalists, politicians, prosecutors, businessmen, and associates.
- **Government line:** Admitted classic wiretapping of Koukakis in some contexts; denied Predator use / Intellexa association — contradicted by technical findings and subsequent prosecutions of vendor-side actors.

### Accountability
`court` `eu-precedent`

- **Oct 2022:** Koukakis sued Intellexa executives for privacy breaches.
- **Jan 2023:** Greek DPA fined Intellexa for obstructing the inquiry.
- **2025–2026:** High-profile Athens trial of Intellexa-linked executives.
- **Feb 2026:** Athens Misdemeanour Court first-instance conviction of Tal Dilian, Sara Hamou, Felix Bitzios, and Yiannis Lavranos for unlawful access / privacy violations — rare EU criminal accountability against commercial spyware principals (sentences reported as cumulative multi-decade figures, suspended pending appeal per Amnesty/ICIJ/EDRi reporting). Civil suits by victim clusters also filed for damages.

## State Apparatus

### EYP (Ethniki Ypiresia Pliroforion)
`state` `domestic-intel` `wiretapping`

- **Role:** Greek National Intelligence Service — domestic and foreign intelligence, lawful intercept under Greek law.
- **Notable:** Central to the wiretapping half of the 2022 scandal (parallel to Predator malware). Leadership resignations and parliamentary fights followed exposure. Predatorgate blurred the line between legal wiretap authority and illegal commercial spyware use.

### Hellenic Police / border security stack
`police` `border` `eu-funds`

- **Role:** Domestic policing plus EU-funded frontier surveillance (sensors, mobile command, data exchange with FRONTEX-adjacent architectures).
- **Notable:** Customer for IDE and European systems vendors; separate track from Predator but part of Greece's full surveillance industrial footprint.

### ADAE / Hellenic Data Protection Authority
`oversight` `dpa`

- **Role:** Communications privacy and data-protection overseers drawn into Predatorgate investigations.
- **Notable:** Institutional friction with the executive over access to surveillance files became part of the scandal's democratic-control story — not just "spyware bad," but "who watches the watchers inside the EU."

## Key Observations

**Full alliance, not one logo.** Intellexa = Cytrox + WiSpear + Nexa + Senpai/Poltrex shells under Dilian/Hamou, with Greek S.A. and Irish holdings — document the stack, not just "Predator."

**Host + buyer + defense base.** Spyware ops (Intellexa), local procurement (Krikel), and defense electronics (IDE/IAI) are three layers of the same Greek security market.

**Rare convictions.** Most spyware scandals end in denial and Entity List paper. Predatorgate produced named criminal convictions of vendor-linked executives — still under appeal, but a template civil society cites.
